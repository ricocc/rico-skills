## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added

- New skill `rico-writer`: Chinese humanized writing, expansion, and de-AI polishing. Preserves facts, author stance, and expression habits while removing template sentences, pseudo-insight markers, anthropomorphic verbs, and generic summaries. Works for articles, tweets, product intros, reviews, and tutorials.
